Bidirectional links are used for walking, not driving navigation. Restricted-access ways and
ways with foot=no are excluded at export. Surface, stairs and turn-by-turn safety are not assessed.

Warehouses, their inventories, the initial responder/incident positions, and community hazard
reports are separate application/demo data. They are not verified OSM relief facilities.
